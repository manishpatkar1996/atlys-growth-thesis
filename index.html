01 · Start with the outcome

Growth must eventually produce more profitable visa transactions.

Many teams influence the outcome. The useful question is not whether conversion or operations matter—they do. It is where a growth team can create new, compounding demand.

This note’s focus: qualified acquisition, travelers per trip, repeat demand and word of mouth. It does not assume the answer is to redesign Atlys’s core visa engine.
02 · A strategic hypothesis

What if the trip—not the application—became the unit of growth?

A visa application is usually part of a larger social and emotional commitment. The trip contains the reason, the group, the constraints, the readiness—and the memory that follows.

Primary unit today

One visa application

One traveler enters with a destination, completes the process and receives the trusted outcome.

Applicant
Opportunity to investigate

One shared trip

One organizer, several travelers, individual requirements, collective readiness—and future trips that retain the context.

This is a direction, not a predetermined product. Atlys does not need to become an OTA or own every travel transaction. Research should reveal which part of the trip Atlys can uniquely organize because it already owns the hardest trust moment.

What research must answer

  • Which decision is painful enough for travelers to change their behavior?
  • Where does Atlys have a real data or trust advantage over search, ChatGPT, Instagram and OTAs?
  • Which entry point generates paid applications—not merely traffic?
  • What is the natural distribution channel for each surface?

Bet two · The travel party

Let one applicant activate the group.

Many trips are coordinated by one person inside WhatsApp, spreadsheets and scattered forms. Atlys already supports multiple travelers. The deeper question is whether companions can become first-class Atlys users—and whether the group can become a natural distribution loop.

What research must answer

  • Which moments already produce recommendations or return behavior?
  • What do travelers actually share privately—and what do they never share publicly?
  • Which outputs create measurable new or repeat paid demand?
  • How can Atlys protect privacy while making useful context reusable?
07 · How I would work

I would begin with travelers—not features.

The opportunity spaces are the conviction. The examples in this note are hypotheses. The exact intervention should be earned through research, behavior and economics.

01 · DISCOVER

Find the real constraint

Interview organizers and companions, map pre-visa decisions, study repeat behavior and inspect where current users already bring others.

02 · TEST

Prove qualified demand

Use concierge tests, landing pages and lightweight flows before investing in deep planning or group infrastructure.

03 · COMPOUND

Scale only the loop

Kill ideas that create vanity traffic. Invest when an output reliably creates another profitable traveler or trip.

The decision gate: real traveler value · an Atlys advantage · natural distribution · conversion proximity · measurable contribution · privacy and trust safety.
