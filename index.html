01 · The growth mandate

Acquire the next traveler through two paths.

One path starts before a person searches for a visa. The other begins after Atlys has delivered a great experience.

A Net-new users

Enter the journey earlier.

Help with a consequential travel decision, then convert qualified intent into Atlys usage.

B Existing travelers

Activate user-led demand.

Use coordination needs and moments of delight to create another user, recommendation or repeat trip.

Demand×Coverage×Acquisition×Conversion×Delivery×Value
The full company equation has six levers. This note deliberately isolates how product-led acquisition, referral and repeat usage can expand completed paid visas.

03 · Path A · Net-new users

Become useful before the visa search.

Today, most travelers reach Atlys after deciding they need a visa. The opportunity is to help while they are still deciding where, when or whether they can travel.

DECIDEWhere can we realistically go?

Passport, residence, budget and group constraints.

TIMECan we make this trip happen?

Leave, events, seasonality and visa timelines.

CONFIDENCEWhat could prevent us from going?

Access, documentation and readiness before money is committed.

These are examples of earlier questions, not a proposed product roadmap.
Earlier travel question → useful Atlys experience → qualified user enters Atlys → visa application
04 · Distribution is part of Path A

A useful experience only grows if travelers find it.

The product and its distribution must be designed together, around places where the earlier question already exists.

05 · Path B · Existing travelers

Let a successful journey create what comes next.

After Atlys delivers value, growth can happen in three ways: the traveler brings someone else, recommends Atlys when it matters, or returns for a future trip.

1Bring another traveler

Research territory: coordination, private invitations or shared trip readiness.

2Recommend Atlys

Research territory: contextual word of mouth, referral or useful travel knowledge.

3Return for another trip

Research territory: reusable context, relevant triggers and next-trip discovery.

Atlys already supports managing multiple travelers. The research question is not how to add one. It is which natural post-experience behavior creates incremental users or trips.

08 · How I would activate the shortlist

Every product bet needs a first audience, a channel and a loop-closing metric.

Coordination wedgeDecision room + Ready-to-Fly board

Distribution: in-product prompts and intent-based CRMMeasure: invited → activated → paid traveler
High-moment wedgeProof, checklist or travel identity

Test one privacy-safe artifact immediately after approval, readiness or trip completion.

Use cohorts, not surveillance. Activate declared interests, consented first-party behavior and explicit community partnerships. Do not infer sensitive habits or private location patterns merely because targeting is technically possible.
