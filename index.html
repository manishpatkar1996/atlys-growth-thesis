Sources and assumptions

  • Atlys Introduction deck, August 2026 — 1M+ applications processed; 45 seconds active input; 99.2% on or before the promised date; verified who/where/when context; stated roadmap across insurance, forex, eSIM and experiences.
  • User-provided Atlys Android screenshots, 26 August 2026 — Explore, Events, approval intelligence, Vault, My Trips, Fast Track and Activities.
  • Yu-kai Chou, Octalysis Framework — motivation lens used to distinguish utility, contribution, identity and social sharing.
  • Reforge, “Growth Loops are the New Funnels” — a loop must reinvest the output of one cycle into the next; the strongest products usually concentrate on one or two major loops.
  • Reforge, “The Racecar Growth Framework” and “You're (Probably) Using NPS Wrong” — separates durable growth engines from optimization and frames word of mouth as a measurable acquisition loop.

All growth products are strategic hypotheses. Prioritization requires Atlys funnel, channel, user-research, operational and contribution-margin data. Word of mouth is treated as both an experience outcome and a partially measurable acquisition source.
