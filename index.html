03 · Opportunity A

Become useful before the visa search.

The bet is not “build a trip planner.” It is to find an earlier travel question that Atlys can answer uniquely—and that leads naturally to a real trip.

DECIDEWhere can we realistically go?

Passport, residence, timing and group constraints.

TIMECan we make this trip happen?

Leave, events, seasonality and visa timelines.

CONFIDENCEWhat could prevent us from going?

Access, documentation and readiness before money is committed.

Earlier question → useful Atlys answer → qualified trip intent → paid visa → better next answer

05 · Opportunity B

Let delivered value create new demand.

Atlys already lets a user manage applications and documents for other travelers. The question is not how to add a traveler. It is how trust creates a new activated user, a recommendation or a future trip.

Do not manufacture sharing.Find the natural action that follows relief, confidence, anticipation or pride.
COORDINATEActivate companions

Possible territory: group planning, private invitations or shared readiness.

HELPEarn recommendations

Possible territory: contextual referrals and verified travel knowledge.

RETURNMake the next trip easier

Possible territory: reusable context, reminders and next-trip discovery.

These are research territories, not feature commitments. The winning mechanism must be discovered.

My pitch

I do not know yet whether the winning answer is planning, coordination, distribution—or something travelers reveal only after we study them.

What I do know is where I would look, what I would measure and how I would turn uncertainty into a focused growth roadmap.

Atlys has earned trust at the hardest moment. I would love to help turn that trust into growth.

Not by guessing the feature. By finding the loop.
